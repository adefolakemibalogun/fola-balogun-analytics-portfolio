HOW I WORK

Trust the data first. Then report it.

I start by agreeing what the data means and who owns it, then test completeness, uniqueness, validity and reconciliation before any number is published. Material exceptions get an accountable owner and are followed through to closure.

The result is information that can be trusted, explained and evidenced, rather than attractive dashboards built on uncontrolled data.

ABOUT

I care whether the numbers can be trusted.

My approach to data is practical and business-led. I like to understand the question behind the numbers, challenge the quality of the underlying data and make sure the final output is something a stakeholder can actually use.

I am drawn to work where data sits close to operations, risk and decision-making, where finding a control gap or a broken definition leads to a lasting fix rather than simply another report.

Across banking and enterprise system change, my focus has become data governance and data quality: clear ownership, agreed definitions, quality rules and evidence, so that reporting can be trusted and decisions made with confidence.
