## Featured Projects

### 1. Banking Operations and Management Reporting

#### Business Problem

Management needed a dependable reporting process for monitoring transaction activity, customer coverage and lending exposure. The analysis also needed to preserve the correct reporting grain so that account, transaction and loan measures were not overstated through inappropriate joins or aggregation.

#### Analytical Solution

The project established a controlled reporting process from raw banking data to management-ready information. It combined data validation, SQL analysis, measure reconciliation and structured reporting across transactions, customers, accounts and loans.

#### Key Findings

* Transaction value declined by **1.8% in 2023** to approximately **£228.8 million**.
* Overdue lending totalled approximately **£1.67 million**, representing **9.8%** of the **£17.09 million** loan portfolio.
* Large Enterprise customers carried the highest total overdue exposure at approximately **£632,000**.
* Small Business customers recorded the highest average overdue facility value at approximately **£59,000**.
* Customers without linked accounts needed to remain visible because excluding them could conceal dormant, incomplete or unresolved customer relationships.

#### Management Actions

* Investigate the 2023 transaction decline by branch, customer segment and transaction type.
* Prioritise concentrated Large Enterprise accounts for exposure monitoring and recovery planning.
* Review high-value Small Business facilities at individual-account level.
* Monitor active, paid-off and overdue lending through recurring management-control reporting.

#### Business Outcome

The completed solution provides a trusted reporting foundation, preserves customer visibility and analytical grain, and enables management to move from portfolio totals to targeted transaction and credit-risk investigation.

### 2. Maven Market Management Information

#### Business Problem

The organisation had strong headline commercial results, but the executive position alone did not explain which customers, products, stores and markets were driving performance. Management also needed to understand whether high sales activity translated into commercial quality after returns.

#### Analytical Solution

A multi-page Power BI management-information solution was developed across Executive, Customer, Product and Geographic Management Packs. The reporting structure connects top-level KPIs with the underlying contribution, concentration and exception drivers.

#### Key Findings

* Revenue reached approximately **£1.20 million**.
* Profit after returns reached approximately **£707,880**.
* A total of **561,065 units** were sold.
* Revenue, profit and unit volume finished approximately **102% above their respective targets**.
* The overall return rate remained controlled at approximately **0.997%**.
* Monarch Rice Medly recorded the highest product-return activity.
* ADJ appeared as the most returned brand.
* Strong headline performance still required investigation to determine whether results were broadly distributed or dependent on a limited number of contributors.

#### Management Actions

* Test the breadth of performance across customers, products, stores and geographic markets.
* Investigate sales activity and return exposure together.
* Assess profitability alongside revenue and unit volume when prioritising products and customer segments.
* Introduce recurring exception reporting with defined thresholds and accountable owners.

#### Business Outcome

The project delivers an integrated management-information framework that connects executive performance with customer, product and geographic drivers. It enables management to move from passive KPI monitoring to exception-led investigation and better-informed commercial prioritisation.

### 3. Evolve Production Intelligence

#### Business Problem

Cumulative oil and gas totals showed the historical scale of field production but did not provide sufficient visibility of current production sustainability, well concentration, water exposure or materially different well-level conditions.

#### Analytical Solution

The project transformed operational production data into three purpose-led Power BI reporting views: Executive Overview, Well Performance and Production Performance Analysis. These views connect field-level results with production trends, well contribution, water cut and on-stream activity.

#### Key Findings

* Oil production totalled approximately **4.73M Sm³** across the reporting period.
* Production peaked earlier in the field life before moving into a sustained lower-output phase.
* Overall field water cut was approximately **49.87%**, making produced-water exposure material to the interpretation of performance.
* Well **15/9-F-12** contributed approximately **4.22M Sm³** of oil, creating substantial dependency on one producing well.
* Secondary wells contributed materially less production and recorded higher water cut.
* Well **15/9-F-4** recorded negligible production during the analysed period and required clarification of its operating status.

#### Management Actions

* Prioritise reliability and performance monitoring for Well 15/9-F-12.
* Investigate wells operating above 50% water cut.
* Clarify the operating condition and future role of Well 15/9-F-4.
* Shift management attention from cumulative production totals towards decline progression, production sustainability and well-level exceptions.

#### Business Outcome

The completed solution creates a structured route from field position to production sustainability, concentration risk, fluid-management exposure and targeted well-level investigation. It supports operational prioritisation while clearly separating analytical indicators from engineering diagnosis.

## Analytical Approach

Each portfolio project follows a consistent decision-focused process:

1. Define the business problem and management questions.
2. Understand, clean and validate the source data.
3. Establish the appropriate data model and analytical grain.
4. Develop and reconcile controlled measures.
5. Design purpose-led reporting views.
6. Interpret the results and identify material findings.
7. Distinguish supported evidence from analytical limitations.
8. Translate findings into management priorities and recommended actions.
